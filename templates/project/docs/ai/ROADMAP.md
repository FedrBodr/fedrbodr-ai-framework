# Roadmap

The roadmap expresses product and engineering direction. It is not a task queue; actionable near-term work belongs in `TODO.md` or the project's issue tracker.

## Now

Committed outcomes currently receiving attention, with evidence of priority.

## Next

Likely outcomes after current work, subject to review.

## Later

Valuable directions without a current commitment.

## Exploring

Questions and experiments intended to reduce uncertainty. Exploration does not promise delivery.

## Explicitly not planned

Ideas intentionally excluded, with a brief rationale to prevent repeated ambiguity.
