# Project Context

Keep this document concise and current. Do not store secrets, credentials, customer records, or sensitive personal data. Link to access-controlled systems when information cannot safely live in Git.

## Product context

What problem does the product solve, and in what domain?

## Users

Who uses or operates the product, and what outcomes matter to them?

## Business goals

List current goals and measurable outcomes. Distinguish verified commitments from hypotheses.

## Current state

Summarize released capabilities, maturity, deployment state, and relevant evidence.

## Constraints

Record deadlines, compatibility needs, policies, resources, and other limits that affect engineering choices.

## Data and risk context

Summarize data sensitivity, exposed surfaces, applicable obligations, business impact of failure, and known risk owners. Keep sensitive details in an approved access-controlled system and link to it safely.

## Active work

Describe the current initiative, owners, expected outcome, and links to detailed tasks.

## Known problems

List confirmed problems, impact, workarounds, and evidence. Avoid presenting assumptions as facts.
