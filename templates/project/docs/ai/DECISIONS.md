# Decision Log

Use this file for small, local, or readily reversible decisions. Use a dedicated ADR for architectural, cross-cutting, costly-to-reverse, or long-lived decisions, and link it here. Do not rewrite a past entry to conceal a changed decision; mark it superseded.

## Entry format

### YYYY-MM-DD — Decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded
- **Context:** What prompted the choice?
- **Decision:** What was chosen?
- **Consequences:** What improves, worsens, or requires follow-up?
- **Owner:** Who approved or owns follow-up?
- **Risk:** What risk is accepted, reduced, or introduced?
- **Links:** Issue, pull request, evidence, or ADR.
